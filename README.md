# 🌊 About AquaGuard

**AquaGuard** is a Python-based community water monitoring prototype developed for **Hack-AI-Thon 2026**.

The system focuses on two important aspects of responsible water management:

> 💧 **Water Quality** — Monitor basic water-quality observations and identify potentially abnormal values.

> 🚰 **Water Usage** — Track daily water consumption and identify potentially excessive or unusual usage.

AquaGuard converts water observations into meaningful information such as:

- 📊 Consumption insights
- 📈 Usage trends
- 🧪 Water-quality status
- ⚠️ Alerts
- 💡 Recommendations
- 📋 Monitoring reports
- 🏠 Location-wise summaries

### 🎯 Our Goal

> **Don't just collect water data — understand it and act on it.**

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Water is one of the most important resources for human life, yet water-related problems can occur at both the **quality** and **consumption** level.

In many homes, schools and communities:

- Water consumption is not regularly monitored.
- Increasing usage may go unnoticed.
- Possible wastage or leakage may not be identified early.
- Basic water-quality observations may remain as isolated numbers.
- Abnormal pH or turbidity readings may not be clearly highlighted.
- Users may not have a simple system connecting observations to recommended actions.
